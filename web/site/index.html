How it works

On air in three steps.

No set-top boxes to configure, no cables to the back office. If the TV can reach the internet, it can run PROVIEW.

1

Pair the TV

Open the PROVIEW app on the TV. It shows a six-character code. Type it into the dashboard and the TV is yours.

2

Build a playlist

Pick a layout, drop in pictures, videos, PDFs or text, add widgets like a clock or price list, and choose when each slide shows.

3

Press Publish

Choose which TVs get it. They download everything first, then switch over within seconds, with no freezing or half-loaded videos.

The TV app

Set it up once. It looks after itself.

  • Downloads before it playsEvery picture and video is saved on the TV first, with a progress bar the first time. Nothing stutters.
  • Keeps playing offlineIf the Wi-Fi drops, the screen carries on with what it already has and catches up when it's back.
  • Starts with the TVSwitch the TV on and the player opens by itself. Staff don't need to touch it.
  • A menu on the remoteReload, check status, change Wi-Fi, update the app or unlink the TV, all from the TV remote.
  • Updates on requestNew versions install from the dashboard with one press. No visit needed.
  • Portrait, tooMount the TV on its side for a tall menu board. The app follows the TV's orientation, or you can rotate it from the dashboard.
The dashboard

Everything on your screens, in one place.

Works on a computer, tablet or phone at app.proviewsignage.co.uk. Changes save as you go, and Publish sends them to your TVs.

Split the screen your way

Choose landscape or portrait, then a layout: full screen, halves, a main area with a side panel, three columns, four squares and more.

  • Drag sliders to resize each area. The exact size shows as you go, e.g. 1080 × 1248 px, so your designer can make artwork that fits perfectly.
  • Add a scrolling ticker at the top, bottom, both, or inside one area.
  • Start from a ready-made template, or from scratch.
  • The preview always shows the whole screen, exactly as it'll look on the TV.

Pictures, videos, PDFs and live widgets

Upload from your phone's camera roll or your computer. Each page of a PDF menu becomes its own slide.

  • Widgets you drag and resize: clock & date, weather, QR code, menu / price list, text box.
  • Transitions (fade, slide, zoom, circle…) and motion effects (slow zoom, pan, flashing…).
  • Fit each picture: stretch, fill, fit whole, or fit with a blurred background.
  • Show slides only on certain dates, days or hours, like breakfast until 11am.

See and control every TV

Each TV has its own page: what's on air now, whether it's online, a live screenshot of exactly what it shows, and its schedule for the week.

  • Different playlists at different times, with priority rules for campaigns like Christmas.
  • Group TVs by site and give each site its own schedule.
  • Reload a TV, update its app or rename it from anywhere.
  • Emergency message: take over some or all screens instantly.

Proof that it played

TVs report what they showed and for how long. Group by playlist, TV, slide or day, and download a CSV for Excel.

  • Activity log of who changed what, and when.
  • Alerts when a TV goes offline or a licence is ending, in the dashboard and by email.
  • Approvals: editors' changes can wait for a manager before they go live.

Get the TV app

Install PROVIEW on your TV.

For Android TV, Google TV and Amazon Fire TV. The app updates itself from the portal once it's installed.

Download TV app (APK) Android 5.1 or newer
Or type this on the TVapp.proviewsignage.co.uk/tv.apk

Amazon Fire TV

  1. From the home screen, search for Downloader and install it (it's free).
  2. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn on Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader, type app.proviewsignage.co.uk/tv.apk and press Go.
  4. Press Install, then Open. The pairing code appears on screen.

Android TV & Google TV

  1. Install Downloader from the Google Play Store on the TV.
  2. Open it, type app.proviewsignage.co.uk/tv.apk and press Go.
  3. When asked, allow Downloader to install apps, then press Install and Open.
  4. Type the pairing code into the portal under Screens → Add a TV.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I start my free trial?

Press Start free trial, create your account and give your business a name. Then install the TV app and pair your TV. The trial is free for 7 days on 1 screen, with no card needed. To add more screens, buy a licence for your first TV. Prefer us to set it up? Call 020 8935 5495.

Which TVs does it work on?

Any TV running Android TV or Google TV (Sony, Philips, TCL, Hisense and many more), and Amazon Fire TV sticks. Have a different TV? Plug a Fire TV stick into its HDMI port. See Get the TV app for how to install it.

What happens if the internet goes down?

The screen keeps playing. Everything is saved on the TV before it plays, so it carries on with your latest content and picks up any changes once it's back online.

Do I need a computer?

No. The dashboard works on a phone or tablet too. You can take a photo, upload it and publish it to your screens from the shop floor.

Can I show different things at different times?

Yes. Schedule playlists or single slides by date, day and hour. For example, breakfast until 11am on weekdays, and a Christmas campaign that overrides everything in December.

Can my staff update the screens?

Yes. Invite them as Editors or Managers. If you'd like to check changes first, turn on approvals and a manager approves before anything goes live.

Is there a contract?

No. You buy a licence for each TV for 1 month, 12 months or 36 months. Your free 7-day trial covers 1 screen.

Can you set it all up for us?

Yes. We can supply ready-to-go TV sticks, install the screens and build your first playlists. Call 020 8935 5495 for a quote.
